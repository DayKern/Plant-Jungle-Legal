§ 15 Final provisions

Should individual provisions of these Terms be or become invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected. The statutory provisions apply in place of the invalid provision.

These Terms are available in German and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the German version prevails.
